\sectionheading{Research Activity}
	
	\organization{The Aggregate Green Elasticity of Substitution (\href{https://ruiagmsousa.github.io/research/agg-green-elasticity}{Draft})}{}{}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2.5em, itemsep=-0.25em, topsep=0.5em]\vspace{-0.3em}
    \item Using a novel semi-structural model, provide the first quantification of the US-wide elasticity of substitution between clean and polluting energy sources -- a key parameter governing energy prices pass-through, and the macroeconomic costs of decarbonization
    \item After deriving a new equilibrium relationship between energy consumption and observable prices, exploit U.S. states' heterogeneous energy mixes to implement a shift-share instrumental variables strategy, estimating an aggregate elasticity of $0.5$
    \item Construct an analytical mapping between sectoral elasticities and their aggregate counterpart. Using 2022 data to calibrate the model, find that energy end-users electrify more easily than the electricity sector shifts to green sources, recovering elasticities of $0.8$ and $0.52$, respectively
\end{itemize}
